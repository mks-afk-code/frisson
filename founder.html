Mikhail Seleznev

Before founding Frisson Writing in 2026, Mikhail spent six years at a leading corporate reporting agency in London and Dubai, leading content development on annual and sustainability reports for companies listed in the GCC and internationally.

His work brings together financial analysis, sustainability reporting and investor messaging. He has interviewed more than 40 board members and senior executives, and those conversations shape every narrative he builds. His projects include the integrated report of du and the annual report of Milaha, Qatar's leading maritime and logistics company.

He also speaks at industry events, leading workshops and panels at MEIRA conferences and chapter meetings, and has judged the ARC Awards and the MEIRA Reporting Awards.
