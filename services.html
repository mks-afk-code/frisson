Services

Frisson Writing provides the content and consultancy behind corporate disclosure and investor communications.

Areas of work

Annual and integrated reportsReporting

Development of new reports and enhancement of existing ones, covering all content including operational, financial and other relevant disclosures.

Sustainability reportsReporting

People-focused sustainability reports that highlight a company's efforts while meeting ESG rating requirements, disclosure rules and international frameworks such as GRI, SASB, the UN SDGs and IFRS S1 and S2.

Includes support with materiality assessments, data verification and preparation for public disclosure.

Governance reportsReporting

Development of governance reports in line with regional and international best practice, local listing rules and the expectations of the global investment community.

Investor communicationsCommunications

Presentations, equity story development and FAQs that keep the company's message to the market consistent.

Leadership messaging and profilesCommunications

Chairman and CEO statements, key messages and company profiles, drafted from interviews with leadership.

Analysis and benchmarkingAdvisory

Messaging proposals, gap analysis against frameworks and exchange requirements, and peer reviews across more than 20 data points.

Competencies

Each project draws on financial, sustainability and regulatory expertise.

  • Investor communication

    Performance narratives and investment cases built from financial and non-financial data, peer benchmarking and market research.

  • Stakeholder engagement

    Interviews with board members and executives, and engagement with internal and external stakeholders for materiality assessments.

  • Financial analysis

    Financial reviews written from quarterly results, earnings calls and sell-side research.

  • Sustainability reporting

    Working knowledge of GRI, SASB, the UN SDGs and IFRS S1 and S2, with integrated thinking across the six capitals.

  • Governance and compliance

    Listing rules and disclosure requirements in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar and Kuwait, plus gap analyses of governance reports.

  • Copywriting and editing

    Leadership statements, financial summaries, market reviews and investor materials written to a consistent standard.
