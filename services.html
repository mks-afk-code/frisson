Services

Frisson Writing covers the full content of a report and the advisory work that shapes it.

What Frisson Writing delivers

Annual reportsCore service

Full content for the front section of the annual report. This covers the theme, structure and leadership statements, then the strategic, operational and financial reviews.

Suitable for first reports after an IPO, full rebuilds and yearly updates.

Sustainability reportsCore service

Sustainability and ESG reports aligned with GRI, SASB, the UN SDGs, IFRS S1 and S2 and local exchange guidance.

Includes support with materiality assessments and data collection.

Integrated reportsCore service

Reports that connect financial and non-financial performance through integrated thinking and the six capitals.

Governance reportsCore service

Copyediting or full drafting of corporate governance reports against the listing rules of the relevant GCC market.

Investor presentations and profilesOn request

Analysis and benchmarkingConsulting

Competencies

Each report draws on financial, sustainability and regulatory expertise.

  • Investor communication

    Performance narratives and investment cases built from financial and non-financial data, peer benchmarking and market research.

  • Stakeholder engagement

    Interviews with board members and executives, and engagement with internal and external stakeholders for materiality assessments.

  • Financial analysis

    Financial reviews written from quarterly results, earnings calls and sell-side research.

  • Sustainability reporting

    Working knowledge of GRI, SASB, the UN SDGs and IFRS S1 and S2, with integrated thinking across the six capitals.

  • Governance and compliance

    Listing rules and disclosure requirements in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar and Kuwait, plus gap analyses of governance reports.

  • Copywriting and editing

    Leadership statements, financial summaries, market reviews and investor materials written to a consistent standard.
